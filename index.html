01 — Two forms of truth

The model estimates.
The market discovers.
The difference is the signal.

Model truth

What an athlete may be worth given everything the system knew at that moment — a fair value built from bounded, inspectable factors, stamped with a model version and an as-of time.

Market truth

What participants are actually willing to pay. A live order book with real mechanics — market and limit orders, shorts with margin, a tape — running on paper cash.

Divergence

When price drifts from fair value, that gap is information. Not a bug to hide — a question to investigate. Star Trade surfaces it in plain language on every athlete.

03 — Explainable by construction

Fair value you can
argue with, factor by factor.

No black box. Every fair value is a transparent product of bounded factors — each one clamped so no single lever can dominate, each one traceable to inputs, news events and a model version.

fair_value= base_value× performance× role× availability× momentum× context× marketability× community

Bounded factors

Each factor responds linearly around a neutral 0.5, then hard-clamps. Form can lift a valuation; it cannot hijack it.

Community, capped at ±5%

The crowd is lexicon-scored, author-weighted and bounded — loud enough to colour a valuation, never loud enough to command it.

Confidence, kept honest

Confidence only widens the uncertainty band. It never moves the central number. Thin data means a wider band, not a quieter model.

Point-in-time everything

Snapshots are immutable, queries respect as-of cutoffs, and digests detect nondeterminism. History stays reconstructible.

04 — The product

Never a trading terminal.
Always a sports story.

The athlete is the emotional and visual centre. Valuation numbers and order tickets come after the story — and full power is one toggle away, never the default.

Discover

Editorial, sports-first home

Athlete stories, hot form and rising attention lead. Prices appear as secondary signals. Market Attention is discussion + news + activity — never raw volume.

Athlete profile

A story in deliberate order

Identity → what's happening now → recent form → community pulse → StarTrade Intelligence → and only then, the trade ticket.

Community

The fan zone, measurable

A lively sports discussion space with reactions, temperature and velocity per athlete. Sentiment scores run quietly in the background — fully auditable, never cosmetic.

Markets

Simple mode first

Buy now / sell now with plain-language divergence. Advanced mode — book, tape, limit orders, time-in-force — sits behind one deliberate toggle.

Rivalries

Athlete-vs-athlete pairs

Currency-pair mechanics for sport. GIANNIS/SHAI, WEMBY/CHET, AMEN/AUSAR: each pair is its own order book whose fair value is the ratio of the two athletes' model fair values — so news, form and sentiment on either side move the pair. The UI leads with both athletes' identities, always.
